## ✦ My Design Process

</div>

```text
Discover
   ↓
Understand the problem & user needs
   ↓
Define
   ↓
Organize flows, structure & requirements
   ↓
Explore
   ↓
Sketch concepts & wireframes
   ↓
Design
   ↓
Create polished high-fidelity interfaces
   ↓
Prototype
   ↓
Build interactions & user journeys
   ↓
Refine
   ↓
```
